## What changed because of what was seen

- **Address pill at large text.** The checker passed, but at 390 × 2.0 the host
  collapsed to `foru…` and at 320 × 2.0 to `f.`: the pill kept every control
  on one line and squeezed the host. Now the pill wraps: the host has a
  flex-basis of `calc(7rem − 40px)` (grows with the text scale), and the route
  badge, reload and shield are one group (`.pend`) that drops to a second line
  together when the host would otherwise go under that width. At 390 × 1.0 the
  pill is one line; at 320 × 1.0 and at 2.0 it is two, with the host whole.
- **In-pill targets.** Reload and the shield were 40 dp circles with −4 dp
  margins, so their hit areas overlapped (centres 36 dp apart). They are now
  48 × 48 with no overlap; the visible host is a few characters shorter at
  390 × 1.0 (`forum.exampl…`).
- **Host meta lines** in the dashboard rows were ellipsized mono; a host was cut
  to `notes.example.org · sock…` at 1.0. They now wrap (`overflow-wrap:
  anywhere`), so the whole value is always readable. Cost at 2.0: breaks inside
  a host (BRIEF copy question 6).
- **Back in `8a`** was a 40 dp-wide target; now 48. (The back and reload icons
  in `8b`/`8c` stay 40 dp wide because they are inert and unlabelled, as the
  inventory says; they are not targets.)
- **Script badges** (`10d`): the JS badge's amber on the tonal fill measured
  **4.19:1**, below 4.5. Badges now sit on `card` with a hairline ring:
  5.73:1 (light) / 8.28:1 (dark). See the table.
- **Chip `×`** in `10e` was a 28 dp hit; now a 48 × 48 hit centred on the
  16 dp icon (negative margins keep the chip's drawn size).
- **Chips and the `N OPEN` pill** are drawn 40 dp tall; each carries an explicit
  48 dp-tall hit area (`::before`), as TOKENS §7 specifies.

## Smallest text and smallest target (measured in the page, 390 × 1.0)

- **Smallest text: 13 px (= 13 sp)** — nav labels (`Sites`), captions,
  badges, `N OPEN`. Nothing is under 13. Measured by walking every element
  with its own text node inside a `.phone` and taking the minimum computed
  `font-size`.
- **Smallest drawn tappable box: 40 dp** — the workspace / route / user-agent
  chips and the `N OPEN` pill, each with a 48 dp hit area as above. Every
  other tappable element (icon buttons, in-pill reload and shield, panic,
  switches' 52 × 48 hit, marker swatches, keypad keys 72 dp or 60 dp under
  640 dp tall, buttons 52 dp, text buttons 48 dp, nav items) measures ≥ 48 dp
  in its smaller dimension.
