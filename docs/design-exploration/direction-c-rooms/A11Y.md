the 20 extended in the brief's order, then `10c` and `9a`): 32 sections, 36
phone frames (`2b` has three, `2a` and `7b` two each).

## What the runs and the screenshots made me change

The overflow checker passed early, but screenshots at 320 × 2.0 showed text that
fit only by breaking mid-word. Fixed:

- **Phone-wide `overflow-wrap: anywhere` → `break-word`.** `anywhere` let flex
  rows shrink every word, so "Toda/y", "Reloa/d", "SOCK/S5". Only values (mono:
  hosts, ports, code) keep `anywhere`, since a long host must still break.
- **Value rows wrap instead of squeezing.** A settings/sheet row with a title
  and a value (`Proxy` · `SOCKS5 · 127.0.0.1:9050`) now wraps the value onto
  its own line, right-aligned, when both do not fit. Switch rows never wrap
  (their text column has `flex-basis: 0`), so the switch stays beside its
  label.
- **Address pill.** Host over route badge in a column; when the column cannot
  get 96 px, reload and shield wrap inside the pill (the pill grows). Badges,
  chips, segment labels and the `N OPEN` pill no longer break inside a word.
- **☰ tiles** (`2b`) reflow from four columns to two at large scales.
- **Sheet children no longer shrink** (`flex-shrink: 0`): at 320 × 2.0 the ☰
  sheet's first group had collapsed to an empty strip.
- **`9a`'s neighbouring card** peeked past the frame (the checker's only
  finding: four spills of 129–146 px); it is now inside it, label ellipsized.
- **Touch targets raised to 48:** the pill's reload / stop / shield (were 44),
  chips and the chip-row `+` (were 40), `10e`'s chip remove × (was 32), the
  reader's `Aa` (was 43.6 wide).
- **Keypad keys are 52 tall when the frame is under 640 high** (TOKENS §3 says
  56 under 640; 52 is the app's own compact height and lets `4a` fit more at
  320 × 568). At 390 × 844 they are 64.

Screenshots looked at with the Read tool after the fixes: `2b` at 320 × 2.0
(three frames), `6c` and `2d` at 320 × 2.0, `1b` at 320 × 1.3 and 390 × 2.0,
`9b` at 320 × 1.3, `10c` at 390 × 1.3 in Ink, `6b` in Ink. All read correctly;
at 2.0 the frames scroll vertically (allowed) and long hosts in Figtree meta
lines still break inside the host (`notes.exampl/e.org`), which is the honest
result of a 26 px meta line in a 200 px column.

## Measured extremes (at 390 × 1.0, every frame except `9a`'s system UI)

Measured with a Playwright script over the rendered page, not asserted:

- **Smallest text: 12 px** (= 12 sp): `T.barBadge` (`SOCKS5`, `WIPES ON EXIT`,
  `PROXY`, `LIVE`), always weight 700, and the file-kind badge (`PDF`, `CSS`,
  `JS`). Everything else is 13 or larger; body is 16. Every text size scales
  with `--scale` except monogram letters and kind badges, which shrink to fit
  their square (capped at 15–21 px and 14 px), as the app's `Monogram` does.
- **Smallest touch target: 48 × 48.** The smallest measured tappable element is
  48 high (chips, `Save`, text buttons, icon taps). Switches are 52 × 32 drawn
  inside rows of at least 56 that toggle as a whole; the panic square is 48; the
  `2c` and `5b` square buttons 52; keypad keys 64 (52 on short frames).

## Contrast

Every pairing the screens use is in `contrast-pairs.txt` and measured by
`tools/contrast.py --table`: **206 pairs, 206 pass, none below 4.5:1 (text) or
3:1 (meaningful marks).** The screens pass added four pairs that TOKENS did not
list, for `8c`'s banner text on the danger tint:

| Pair | FG | BG | Ratio | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| light: ink text on dangerTint | `#1E1B16` | `#F7E0DA` | 13.59:1 | 4.5:1 |
| light: ink2 text on dangerTint | `#4A453C` | `#F7E0DA` | 7.53:1 | 4.5:1 |
| dark: ink text on dangerTint | `#F2EDE3` | `#3B201C` | 12.77:1 | 4.5:1 |
| dark: ink2 text on dangerTint | `#CEC7B9` | `#3B201C` | 8.87:1 | 4.5:1 |

They are appended to `contrast-pairs.txt`; TOKENS.md's table (202 pairs) is
otherwise unchanged.

Not measured, by design: the placeholder web page behind the chrome
(decorative), the scrim-dimmed content under a sheet, inert controls at 38 %
(exempt under WCAG 1.4.3 / 1.4.11), and `9a`, which Android draws.

## Copy audit

Every text node inside a phone frame was checked against the two inventory
files. The only strings not found literally are the inventory's own templates
with sample data filled in (`4 OPEN`, `4 OPEN SESSIONS`, `background · 3 min`,
`<host> · direct` for the decoy sites, `3 sessions destroyed, …`, `8b`'s detail
with `Forum` and `socks5 · 127.0.0.1:9050`), `6c`'s per-category counts (sample
figures summing to the canvas's 164), and `10e`'s line numbers. No string was
invented. Case changes are CSS only.

## Deviations worth a reviewer's eye

- **`6a`'s solid pill is "Keep blocked"** (the canvas, authoritative per the
  inventory), not the code's "Allow once". Button order unchanged.
- **`3c`'s Unlock and `10a`/`10d`'s New workspace / New script** are the
  screen's one solid ink pill (the app draws them neutral or as jade text rows).
- **`5b`'s `+`** is a solid ink square, the Rooms equivalent of the app's jade
  `+` on an empty workspace.
