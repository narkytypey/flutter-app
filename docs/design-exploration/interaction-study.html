Instrument Motion Study

Six transitions of restyle v2, "Instrument" (direction B, the debate's pick), playing on a loop inside phone frames. The source is docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-restyle-v2-design.md §7. Two moments move: unlock, and a protective change. Everything else is an effect with no bounce. Under reduced motion (Flutter's MediaQuery.disableAnimations) both moments become 100 ms cross-fades.

Text inside the frames is the canvas's copy, or the app's where the canvas has none. Everything around the frames is the study's own notes.

Touch marker. The study draws it; the app does not. Placeholder content (no real page or screen text)

What this study cannot show

  • New identity and a route change from 6c's Proxy row trigger the same moment as study 02. Only the pill's side is drawn here, not the confirm sheet or the form.
  • Toggles (§5's 52 × 32 switch) move "as built", by Flutter's default. The spec gives no number for them, so none is shown.
  • The pill growing when a long host wraps happens in layout, with no animation. Study 02 uses a host that fits on one line.
  • Haptics and the system's own transitions (the Android back gesture, the keyboard) are outside the spec.
  • Browser timing is not Flutter timing. CSS transitions approximate Curves.easeOutCubic and Material's decelerate curve, and the loops run on timers.
